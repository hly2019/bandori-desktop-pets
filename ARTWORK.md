## Unofficial fan project

Takamatsu Tomori from the BanG Dream! series. It is a free, noncommercial fan project. It is not affiliated
with, sponsored by, or endorsed by BanG Dream!, Bushiroad, or their partners.

## How the assets were made

The custom pet artwork was created using AI image generation guided by character
references and user feedback, followed by iterative prompt revisions, frame
selection, transparency processing, alignment, animation assembly, and visual
review. These assets are AI-assisted fan adaptations.

The `pets/*/sources/strips/` folders preserve selected generated source artwork.
The `prompt-records/` folders retain original generation and revision prompts in
the language used during production. These records document the process; they
do not guarantee pixel-identical reproduction from a prompt alone. The preview
GIFs and videos were assembled from the custom pet frames.
